## How corroboration is scored

Three independent signals decide which entries appear here. They measure
*evidence that a passage really is about its ayah*, nothing more:

| signal | reading |
|---|---|
| quoted run | 4 consecutive words of the ayah also occur in the passage, after orthography folding |
| shingle coverage | share of the ayah's 8-rune windows occurring in the passage; 0.50 or more counts as quoted |
| verse introduction | passage opens with a stock formula such as `يخبر تعالى`, `يقول تعالى`, `قال تعالى` |

A low score means *read it*, never *it is wrong*: paraphrase is a legitimate
way to write Tafsir. It is also why this sheet can be short and still be
complete — the entries below are the ones no signal could vouch for.
